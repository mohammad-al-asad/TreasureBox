FULL DESCRIPTION
Treasure Box turns your city into a real-world treasure hunt.

Every expedition is a real treasure hidden at a real location. Read the story, follow the clues, crack the cipher and be the first explorer to find it.

HOW IT WORKS
• Browse live expeditions – see each hunt's difficulty, category and reward.
• Read the field brief – the story, photos and a preview of the cipher lock.
• Join the expedition – unlock the full set of clues for that hunt.
• Crack the cipher – turn the brass dials to match the markings you find in the field.
• Follow the hints – photo clues and directions guide you closer.
• Navigate – open the location in Google Maps or Apple Maps with one tap.
• Confirm your find – take a photo of the treasure with the in-app camera and claim your discovery.

Each treasure can only be found once. When an explorer confirms the find, the hunt closes and moves to the finished list, so speed and sharp eyes matter.

FEATURES
• Live expeditions that update in real time
• Easy, Medium and Hard hunts
• Tactile brass cipher lock with haptic feedback
• Photo hints and map navigation
• In-app camera to confirm your discovery
• Full English and Arabic support, including right-to-left layout
• Your profile and history of completed hunts

Grab your phone, gather your friends and start exploring. The next treasure is waiting.
