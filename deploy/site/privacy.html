Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Treasure Box mobile app and website ("Treasure Box", "we", "us") collect, why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices you have. By using Treasure Box you agree to this policy.

In short: we collect only what we need to run your account and the treasure hunts. We do not sell your personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not track your location in the background.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

DataWhy we need it
Name and email addressTo create and secure your account, sign you in and contact you about your account, purchases and claims.
PasswordTo sign you in. We only store it in encrypted (hashed) form and can never see it.
Phone number (optional)Only if you add it, to help verify a claim or contact you about a prize.
Profile photo (optional)Shown on your profile if you choose to upload one.
Find photo and finder nameWhen you confirm that you found a treasure, you take a photo of it and enter a name. We use these to verify the claim and record the finder of that hunt.
Messages to supportTo answer your questions.

Information created when you use the app

  • Hunt activity: which hunts you joined or bought, your cipher attempts, and your claims.
  • Purchases: the hunt you paid for, the amount and the PayPal transaction reference. Payments are handled by PayPal. We never receive or store your card or bank details.
  • Technical data: our servers keep short-term logs (such as IP address, device type and time of request) to keep the service secure and fix problems.

Stored on your device

The app keeps your sign-in session and language choice on your phone so you stay signed in. This data stays on your device and is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

Device permissions

  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to take or pick a photo (for a find confirmation or your profile picture).
  • Location: the app does not read your device's location. When you tap "Open in Maps", the hunt location opens in Google Maps or Apple Maps, which have their own privacy policies.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the app: accounts, hunts, hints, ciphers, purchases and claims.
  • To check that a claimed find is real and to award prizes.
  • To send account emails such as password reset codes and claim updates.
  • To keep Treasure Box safe, prevent fraud and cheating, and fix bugs.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run Treasure Box, and only as needed:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) – hosts our servers and stores uploaded photos (Stockholm, Sweden region).
  • MongoDB Atlas – hosts our database.
  • PayPal – processes payments under PayPal's privacy policy.
  • Email providers (Resend or Google) – deliver account emails.
  • Google Maps – shows map previews of hunt areas under Google's privacy policy.

When a hunt is found, the finder name you entered may be shown in the app as the person who found it. We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of our users, or as part of a business transfer.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information within 30 days. We may keep purchase and claim records longer where the law requires it (for example for tax or prize records), and server logs are deleted automatically after a short period.

5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and uploaded photos are stored privately with time-limited access links. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can see and update your name and photo in the app's Profile screen.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it by emailing support@treassurebox.com.
  • You can delete your account at any time. See Delete your account.

7. Deleting your account

To delete your account and the data linked to it, follow the steps on our account deletion page.

8. Children

Treasure Box is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Some hunts and prizes may have a higher age limit shown in the hunt rules. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date. If the changes are significant, we will let you know in the app.

10. Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email support@treassurebox.com.
