Delete your account

For the Treasure Box app on Android and iPhone

You can ask us to delete your Treasure Box account and the personal data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@treassurebox.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Treasure Box, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We will confirm by email and delete your account within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your name, email address, phone number and password
  • Your profile photo
  • Your hunt progress and cipher attempts
  • Photos you uploaded with claims that were not approved

What we may keep

  • Payment records (amount, date, PayPal reference) for up to 5 years, where tax and accounting laws require it.
  • Approved prize claims (finder name and proof photo) for up to 5 years, as a record of who won each prize.
  • Short-term server logs, which are deleted automatically.

Anything we keep is used only for these legal reasons and is deleted when it is no longer needed.

See our Privacy Policy for more details.
